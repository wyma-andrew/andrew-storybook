Establishing · Wide
Wide low-angle establishing shot: 19th-century basalt lighthouse on a storm-lashed headland, amber Fresnel beam cutting through heavy rain, rusted gallery rail, sea spray across frame. Night, cold blue-black palette, single warm source. Photorealistic, anamorphic.

Medium shot of Mira Kade — 34, cropped auburn hair, salt-stained oilskin coat — wiping a 1890s Fresnel optic with a worn rag. Amber lamp light rakes her face, rain-streaked glass behind. 50mm, shallow DOF, muted North Atlantic palette, photorealistic.
